How it works

Build there. Manage here.

01 · CREATE

Build on AuraPops

Customers create and customize their popup directly on aurapops.online.

02 · REQUEST

Send activation request

The saved AuraPop enters the AuraDigital request queue automatically.

03 · APPROVE

Confirm & activate

Payment and activation are handled from the main AuraDigital admin dashboard.

04 · LIVE

QR goes live

Once approved, the public AuraPop becomes available at aurapops.online/pops/…
