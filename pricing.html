Before you start

A QUOTE YOU
CAN UNDERSTAND.

What is included?

The agreed pages and features, responsive implementation and launch checks. Content, integrations and maintenance are clarified during review.

How do payment stages work?

Small projects normally use a 50% deposit and 50% final payment. Larger projects can use 30% / 40% / 30%. We can adjust the stages in your quote.

Is the estimate the final price?

Simple configurations show an instant starting price. Additional features produce an estimate; complex workflows require a custom quote. We review scope before agreement.

Can I get ongoing growth support?

Yes. Our existing weekly and monthly growth packages remain available.
