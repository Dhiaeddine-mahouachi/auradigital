STORY 01 MOVING & LOGISTICS / ISTANBUL

A move is a big decision.
The next step should be simple.

For someone preparing a move, clarity comes first: what can be transported, where does the company operate, and how do you ask for a quote?

THE BUSINESS

Mutlu Nakliyat offers local and intercity moving, office relocation, and smaller-item transport.

WHAT WE BUILT

A mobile-friendly service website with dedicated moving information, a quote call-to-action, and clear contact options.

WHAT A VISITOR CAN DO

Explore the service types, read about transport options, and contact the business to request a quote.

STORY 02 AUTO ELECTRICAL / FATIH

When something breaks,
people need answers fast.

A driver with a battery, air conditioning, or electrical problem wants to know whether a workshop can help — and how to reach it.

THE BUSINESS

Yurt Oto Elektrik lists battery replacement, air-conditioning service, electrical diagnostics, and starter/charging repairs.

WHAT WE BUILT

A service-led website that organizes technical expertise into understandable categories, with clear call and directions actions.

WHAT A VISITOR CAN DO

Check the relevant service, find the workshop in Fatih, and make contact without navigating a complicated page.
