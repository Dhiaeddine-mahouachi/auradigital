Rules

Do and don’t.

Do

  • One primary button per screen: “Start your path”.
  • Yellow for one highlight per screen only.
  • Show the product (demo UI), never stock photos.
  • Headline ≤ 8 words, one supporting line, no paragraphs on the homepage.
  • Only tokens from aura-ds.css: no inline colors, sizes or shadows.

Don’t

  • Pastel backgrounds per card, or more than 3 colors on a screen.
  • The italic serif outside the hero.
  • Empty states, test accounts or real names in product visuals.
  • Ticking countdowns, pop-up bubbles on load, mixed icon styles.
  • Invented testimonials or numbers.
