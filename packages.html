Frequently asked questions

Before we start, let's make it clear.

Is there a commitment?

Scope and duration are defined in the proposal. Care packages are monthly subscriptions; short-term custom work is quoted separately.

Is ad spend included?

No. AuraDigital handles management and optimization; Meta/Google media spend comes from the business's ad budget.

Are hosting and domains included?

No, hosting and domain renewals are separate from Website Care plans unless a proposal explicitly includes them.

What if I already have a website?

A new site is not required. We can review your current setup and continue with landing pages, updates and optimization.
