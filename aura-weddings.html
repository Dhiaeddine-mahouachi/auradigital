A FEW BEAUTIFUL DETAILS

Before your
first invitation.

Can we edit after publishing?

Yes. Reopen your saved invitation in the same browser, change your details, and save. Your published link uses the updated details.

Which languages are supported?

The Signature Collection supports Arabic and English, including RTL layout and local dates. The Classic Collection also offers French and Turkish previews.

Can we choose our music?

Yes. Add an HTTPS link to a recording you own or are licensed to share. Music starts when the guest presses play. Original ambient previews are included in demos; commercial Arabic songs are not bundled.

How do RSVPs and guest limits work?

Published signature invitations store responses securely. You can set a maximum party size, create personal guest links with smaller limits, and export an attendance list. A repeat response from the same guest link updates the earlier one.

Who can see our guests’ details?

RSVP details are available to the invitation owner and authorized AuraDigital editors. Public visitors see only approved guestbook messages and approved photographs.

Can guests upload wedding photographs?

Yes. JPEG, PNG and WebP photographs up to 10 MB are resized before upload. You review them in the studio before publication. The initial photo wall holds up to 100 uploads per invitation.

Can we share through WhatsApp or QR?

After publication, the studio provides a link, QR code, WhatsApp sharing action and downloadable cover. Private invitations require personal guest links.

What about pricing, expiry and hosting?

Request a quote for the service and agree on hosting and duration with AuraDigital before ordering. No payment is processed in this studio and no hosting duration is promised here.

How do we remove our data?

The studio lets you delete your invitation and its guest data. Keep access to the browser where you created your draft; customer account recovery is not yet available.
