Questions

Can we edit after publishing?

Yes. Reopen your saved invitation, change your details and save — your link shows the update.

Which languages are supported?

Signature designs support Arabic and English with right-to-left layout. Classic designs also offer French and Turkish.

Can we choose our music?

Yes. Add a link to a recording you own or are licensed to share. It plays only when the guest presses play.

How do RSVPs work?

Responses are stored securely. Set a maximum party size, create personal guest links and export your attendance list.

Who can see our guests’ details?

Only you and authorized AuraDigital editors. Visitors see approved guestbook messages and photos only.
