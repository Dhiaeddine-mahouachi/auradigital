# Royal Champagne assets

`couple.webp`, `venue.webp`, `florals.webp`: generated using the built-in image generator, with 01_PRIMARY_DESIGN_EXACT.png as the visual reference. They are new illustrative assets; the venue does not depict an identified real hotel and the couple is fictional. Original generated PNG files were inspected then optimized to WebP, preserving floral transparency.

Generation prompts: recreate the back-facing couple in the amber chandelier palace (panel 4); create an ivory/blush rose and magnolia corner with gold botanical filigree on transparency (panels 3/7); recreate a symmetrical gold-lit palace approach with palms and white flowers (panel 6). No text, interface, phone frames or full-page screenshots.

`paper-grain.svg`: native SVG fractal paper texture.
`oud-preview.wav`: original digitally synthesized 48-second Hijaz instrumental, generated by `scripts/generate-royal-audio.py`. No third-party samples/recordings. Dedicated to CC0. It is an oud-style plucked-string/reed synthesis, not a live instrumental recording.

Fonts and their OFL licenses are stored in ../fonts.
